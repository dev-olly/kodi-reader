# Kodi Reader 0.4.5

- Add multiple sketches among the paragraphs of a note. Type `/sketch` or `/draw` at the start of a paragraph, then press **Enter** or click **Create sketch** in the cursor suggestion. **Escape** dismisses the suggestion.
- Saved sketches appear as preview cards. Click a card to edit its drawing, or use **Draw · Sketches** to browse, rename, create, and delete sketches. **Back to note** returns to your writing; **Command–Enter** finishes editing the note.
- Use sketches in both sidebar and window notes, with support for Write, Markdown Source, and Preview modes. Existing drawings migrate into the first sketch automatically.
- Keep sketch scenes separate, refresh previews after edits, and retain unsaved changes for retry when saving fails. Removing the last reference deletes its sketch; Undo restores it while the note is open.
- Include sketch images in Word and Apple Notes HTML exports. Markdown and Notion exports include a companion assets folder; keep it beside the exported document.
- Sync individual sketches and their previews with their notes. Update Kodi Reader on every Mac before editing notes with multiple sketches across devices.

The app and update feed are signed, and the download is notarized by Apple. Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later.
