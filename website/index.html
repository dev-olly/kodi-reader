04 / AUDIO

Keep the thought.
In your own voice.

Type /audio in a note and talk it through. Record, pause, resume, and keep a player beside the passage. Seek through the waveform, rename the recording, or export it as an audio file.

Turn a recording into a transcript, then expand or copy it. Mac uses on-device speech in supported languages. Windows and Linux editions use downloadable Whisper transcription and PocketTTS voices, then work offline. Recordings and saved transcripts travel with your note exports.

A QUIETER KIND OF READER

Close to the book.
Close to you.

01 / ON YOUR DEVICE

Your thoughts stay close.

Reading and local notes work without an account. Write, sketch, record, and save research topics offline. Download the speech models once, then listen and transcribe on your device.

02 / BETWEEN DESKTOPS

Carry the margin with you.

Optional Google Drive sync connects books and compatible notes across desktop editions. Mac also offers iCloud. Sketches, recordings, transcripts, and reminders can travel with your notes; sync is off by default.

03 / YOUR CALL

A free reader. A choice to ask.

The core app is free and open source. Ask AI is a separate, opt-in online feature for conversations about your book. See how Ask AI works ↗
