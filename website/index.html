04 / AUDIO

Keep the thought.
In your own voice.

Type /audio in a note and talk it through. Record, pause, resume, and keep a player beside the passage. Seek through the waveform, rename the recording, or export it as an audio file.

When macOS supports your current language, turn a recording into text on your Mac, then expand or copy it. Recordings and saved transcripts travel with your note exports.

Close to you.

01 / ON YOUR MAC

Your thoughts stay close.

Read, write, sketch, record, and save research topics offline. EPUB listening runs locally after its voice and pronunciation files download. On-device transcription may need a macOS speech-model download and a supported language.

02 / ACROSS YOUR MACS

Carry the margin with you.

Choose optional iCloud or Google Drive sync for your books and notes, including sketches, audio, saved transcripts, and reminders. You decide what travels; sync is off by default.

03 / YOUR CALL

A free reader. A choice to ask.

The core app is free and open source. Ask AI is a separate, opt-in online feature for conversations about your book. See how Ask AI works ↗
