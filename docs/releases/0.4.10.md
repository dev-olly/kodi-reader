# Kodi Reader 0.4.10

- Listen with a new audio player designed for Kodi, including chapter progress,
  15-second skips, playback speed, narrator selection, and optional text following.
  Narration continues into the next chapter.
- Select a passage and choose **Listen from Here** to start listening at that
  passage and continue through the book.
- Follow footnotes, citations, and other EPUB references, then use **Back to
  reading** to return to your original passage. Nested references return one step
  at a time, even after changing the font, window size, or column layout.
- Pause narration automatically when following a reference. Audio stays paused
  during the detour and after returning, including when speech is still buffering,
  until you press Play.
- Keep spoken passages distinct with a subtle underline that works alongside
  saved highlights and text selection. Avoid coloring ordinary text as links.
- Keep page turns aligned when scrolling rapidly, and preserve your passage when
  opening or closing the audio player.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later. Includes iCloud and Google Drive
sync support. Narration is available for English EPUBs and runs on your Mac after
the optional voice files have downloaded.
