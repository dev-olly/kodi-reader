# Kodi Reader 0.4.12

- A new Mac library with a quiet green sidebar, spacious cover cards, reading
  filters, search, sorting, and grid or list views.
- Browse highlights and notes across your library, then open a note directly in
  its source book. Reach research reminders and settings from the sidebar.
- Save a public webpage as an offline article to read and annotate in Kodi.
- Ask AI now gives short, simple, direct answers by default, with more detail or
  examples when you ask for them.
- Refreshed audio-note recording and playback cards, with an option to add a
  transcript to your note.
- Improve highlight actions, opening notes, and switching between reading
  panels while preserving live edits and your reading position.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later. Includes iCloud and Google Drive
sync support.
