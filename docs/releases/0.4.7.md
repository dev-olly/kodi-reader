# Kodi Reader 0.4.7

This update restores iCloud sync for installations of 0.4.6 that shipped without iCloud support.

- Existing iCloud sync settings work again, and pending local changes can resume syncing.
- Release packaging now requires iCloud support and validates Production CloudKit permissions in the final app.
- Published release checks also verify iCloud support in the downloadable disk image.

Your local books, notes, reading progress, and pending changes are preserved. No library reset or reimport is required.

The app and update feed are signed, and the download is notarized by Apple. Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later.
