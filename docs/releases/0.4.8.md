# Kodi Reader 0.4.8

- Set a daily reading goal in Settings → Reading. Your goal and streak stay in
  Settings by default; turn on Show reading goal and streak to display them on
  Home and in the reader toolbar. Reading history stays on
  this Mac and works with usage analytics turned off.
- Keep highlight actions inside the reading area, including in narrow windows
  and near the bottom of a page. Clear plain highlights or delete annotations
  with notes and drawings using clearer action labels.
- Place reading markers more reliably around wrapped and hyphenated text,
  line beginnings, and column boundaries.
- Share limited usage analytics, with a new Settings → Privacy control to turn
  sharing off and clear unsent events. Book content, notes, account details,
  and AI questions and answers are excluded.
- Improve cleanup of duplicate Kodi Reader and legacy Folio entries in Finder's
  Open With menu, including development builds and failed packaging outputs.
- Open Reading, Privacy, and Updates settings without first looking up Google
  Drive credentials.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later. Includes iCloud and Google Drive
sync support.
