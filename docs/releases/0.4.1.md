# Kodi Reader 0.4.1

This release adds optional iCloud sync so you can continue reading on another Mac.

- Choose **Off**, **Notes only**, or **Books and notes** in Settings → iCloud. Sync is off until you enable it on each Mac.
- Notes only syncs annotations, drawings, bookmarks, reading position, and book information. Import or locate the matching book file on each Mac.
- Books and notes also syncs imported EPUBs, PDFs, and saved webpages. Books appear in the library before their files are downloaded; open one or choose **Download for Offline Reading** when you need it.
- **Sync Ask AI history** is a separate opt-in for saved conversations and passage references. Drafts and account details stay local.
- Sync status and **Sync Now** are available in Settings. You can keep reading and editing offline; pending changes resume when iCloud is available.

Your private iCloud storage holds synced content and counts against your iCloud allowance. Kodi Reader does not require a separate sync account. Existing local books and notes remain available with sync off.

This release is ready for cross-device testing. Before relying on sync as your only copy, complete the [two-Mac test checklist](https://github.com/dev-olly/kodi-reader/blob/main/docs/icloud-two-mac-test.md).

The app and update feed are signed, and the download is notarized by Apple. Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later.
