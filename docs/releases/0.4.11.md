# Kodi Reader 0.4.11

- Improve Ask AI performance reporting with total response time, latency until
  the first response text, and streaming duration.
- Keep completed, failed, and stopped request timings separate, including when
  switching or closing a book during a response.
- Include these measurements in the existing optional usage analytics. Questions,
  answers, book content, and account details remain excluded. Turn sharing off in
  Settings → Privacy.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later. Includes iCloud and Google Drive
sync support.
