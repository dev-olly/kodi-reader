Purchase information

A plain-language guide to what you receive, when credits are charged, and what happens after payment.

What you are buying

Kodi Reader itself is free to use for reading. Every verified account receives 25 Ask AI credits once. After those are used, you may buy a fixed pack of 250, 600, or 1,400 credits at the prices shown on the pricing page.

One credit pays for one Ask AI answer, including each follow-up. Purchased credits do not expire. Each pack is a one-time purchase with no subscription, renewal, or automatic top-up.

Payment and delivery

Paddle processes checkout as merchant of record. Prices include applicable tax. Credits belong to the Kodi Reader account used to start checkout and are added only after our server receives and verifies Paddle's payment confirmation.

After checkout, the browser asks to return to Kodi Reader. The app checks the order automatically and shows the updated balance after confirmation. If the app does not reopen, return to it manually; the pending order is saved to your account and can still be confirmed.

When a credit is used

We reserve one credit when your question starts. A service failure or cancellation before answer text begins returns the credit. Stopping after answer text begins uses one credit. An interrupted reservation that cannot be settled is released automatically after five minutes.

Refunds, disputes, and deletion

Approved refunds and payment reversals remove the corresponding purchased credits. Credits already used can create a negative adjustment that later purchases first offset. Read the complete Refund Policy.

Deleting your account removes access to unused credits and does not itself request a refund. Contact us about unused purchased credits before deleting your account. A payment completed after deletion is held for review rather than recreating the account.

Compatibility and availability

Ask AI requires a supported Kodi Reader version, a verified account, an internet connection, and available credits. Reading, annotations, and local history remain available without buying credits. AI answers may contain mistakes and should be checked against the original text and reliable sources.

Support

For payment problems, refund requests, or a payment made after deleting your account, email olly@kodi-reader.app with the Paddle receipt reference. Do not send passwords, login codes, or card details.
