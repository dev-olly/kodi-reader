# Kodi Reader 0.4.4

- Clean up older Kodi Reader entries in Finder’s **Open With** menu when the installed app launches, including after an update.
- Keep development builds and leftover packaging copies out of **Open With**.
- Updates replace the previous installed release while preserving your books, notes, drawings, bookmarks, conversations, and reading position.

The app and update feed are signed, and the download is notarized by Apple. Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later.
