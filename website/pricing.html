One credit, one answer

A new question or follow-up uses one credit when Ask AI begins returning an answer.

Reading stays free

Your library, annotations, drawing tools, and existing local conversations remain available without purchasing credits.

No recurring charge

There is no subscription, renewal, trial conversion, or automatic top-up.

Delivered to your account

Credits appear after Paddle confirms payment and can be used on another Mac after you sign in to the same account.

When is a credit returned?

A service failure or cancellation before answer text begins returns the reserved credit. Stopping an answer after text begins uses one credit. An interrupted reservation that cannot be settled is released automatically after five minutes.
