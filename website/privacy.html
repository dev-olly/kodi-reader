Privacy Policy

Kodi Reader keeps reading data on your Mac by default. This policy explains optional cloud features, accounts, Ask AI, and limited app usage analytics.

Updated 7 November 2026

Controller and contact

Kodi Reader is operated under the Kodi Reader brand by an individual sole trader based in Germany. The operator is responsible for the account, Ask AI, and credit data described here. Privacy questions and rights requests may be sent to olly@kodi-reader.app.

Reading data on your Mac

Your imported books, saved webpages, annotations, drawings, research reminders, audio recordings and saved transcripts, reading position, daily reading time and streak history, and saved conversations are stored locally on your Mac. Daily reading history stays on this Mac and works with usage analytics turned off. You can use the reader without a Kodi Reader account.

Audio notes use your microphone when you choose to record and allow microphone access. Transcription runs on your Mac using supported on-device speech recognition; recordings are not uploaded for transcription. Availability depends on the language and speech support on your Mac. macOS may ask for Speech Recognition permission or download a speech model before transcription can run. Read aloud also runs on your Mac, currently for English EPUB text, after downloading its voice files on first use. These local audio features do not require Ask AI credits.

Optional sync uses either your private Apple iCloud storage or a hidden Kodi app-data folder in your Google Drive account. The selected provider's data uses your iCloud or Google storage allowance. Notes-only sync includes annotations, drawings, research reminders, audio recordings and saved transcripts, bookmarks, reading position, and book metadata. Books-and-notes sync also includes imported EPUBs, PDFs, and saved webpages. Ask AI conversations and passage references sync only when you enable Ask AI history sync. Sign-in credentials, credit balances, and unsent drafts are not included. Sync choices apply separately to each Mac. Only one cloud provider syncs on a Mac at a time; switching requires a confirmed copy and leaves the previous cloud copy in place. Google Drive retains immutable historical revisions and chunks so an offline device can recover concurrent edits; deleting Kodi's Google app-data folder clears that history.

Account and authentication data

When you create an account or sign in, Supabase processes your account identifier, email address, verification status, session information, and authentication events. Purelymail delivers email verification codes. If enabled and selected, Google or Apple processes its sign-in flow and provides the identifiers needed to sign you in; Apple may provide a private relay email. Google Drive sync requests separate permission limited to the app-data folder. Session credentials and the Google Drive refresh token are stored in macOS Keychain.

App usage analytics

New releases share limited usage analytics by default to help us understand how many devices use Kodi Reader and which features they use. Events include a random installation identifier, a foreground-activation identifier, timestamp, app version, document format, engagement duration, and an event type such as opening the app, creating or editing a note or drawing, or sending an Ask AI request. Reader engagement includes reading, notes, drawing, and Ask AI in the active reader workspace; time pauses when the workspace is hidden, minimized, asleep, or idle for two minutes. AI outcomes use a separate random telemetry request identifier and a general error category, without linking to billing request identifiers or your Kodi account.

Feature events also count reminder actions, drawing opens and deletions, microphone recording, and text-to-speech listening. Recording and listening include starts, pauses, resumes, relevant outcomes, and time while recording or playback is active. Pauses, permission prompts, model downloads, and speech preparation do not count toward these audio durations. Audio that continues in the background can count separately from foreground reader engagement.

Analytics events do not contain book titles, authors, book identifiers, passages, questions, answers, note text, drawings, reminder titles or text, audio recordings, transcripts, spoken text, email addresses, webpage URLs, or sign-in tokens. They are pseudonymous: the random installation identifier allows us to recognize repeat use of the same installation. Our analytics database does not store IP addresses; hosting providers may still process network metadata to deliver and secure requests.

When events upload, Vercel provides an approximate country inferred from the network connection. We store only the country code with the upload and show aggregate country totals. We do not request GPS access or store a city, precise location, or raw IP address in analytics. VPNs and delayed uploads can affect this country observation; it does not identify where earlier offline activity occurred.

A brief non-blocking notice explains collection in the app. You can turn off Share usage analytics in Settings → Privacy. This stops further collection, removes unsent events, and resets the installation identifier. Already received events remain subject to the retention policy. Disabling analytics does not affect reading, notes, drawings, Ask AI, or your local reading goals. Only aggregate statistics are available in the protected administrator dashboard.

Ask AI data

When you use Ask AI, your question, selected passages, relevant conversation history, and limited book context are sent through our server to OpenAI to generate the answer. Kodi Reader's credit ledger stores request identifiers, status, token-usage counts, and credit movements, but does not store your question, answer, book passage, or login token.

We do not intentionally write emails, questions, book passages, login codes, or access tokens to ordinary server request logs. Service providers may process operational and security metadata according to their own policies.

Credits and payments

Supabase stores your credit balance, welcome-credit status, reservations, settlements, purchase entitlement, request identifiers, and token-usage counts. Paddle processes checkout, payment, receipts, tax, refunds, and disputes as merchant of record. We receive Paddle customer and transaction identifiers, purchase details, status, amount, currency, and adjustment information so we can deliver or reverse credits. Kodi Reader does not receive or store complete card details.

Website data

The public website is hosted by Vercel, which may process IP addresses, request headers, timestamps, and security or delivery logs. Kodi Reader does not use advertising trackers or sell personal data. The marketing and legal pages do not set Kodi Reader analytics cookies. Paddle's checkout script is loaded only on the checkout page when checkout is enabled and may process data required to provide payment services.

Why we process data

Where the GDPR applies, processing is based on:

  • performance of a contract, to authenticate you, answer questions, manage credits, deliver purchases, and provide support;
  • legitimate interests, to secure the service, prevent fraud and duplicate grants, diagnose failures, enforce limits, and maintain reliable records;
  • your consent, where a feature or provider specifically asks for it and consent is the applicable basis;
  • legal obligations, including tax, accounting, consumer-protection, and lawful-request requirements.

Service providers and international processing

We use Supabase for authentication and database services, OpenAI for AI responses, Purelymail for email delivery, Paddle for payments and tax handling, Vercel for website hosting, and Fly.io for backend hosting. Optional Apple iCloud, Google Drive, Sign in with Apple, or Google sign-in is used only when you enable or choose it. These providers may process data in other countries using the safeguards described in their own privacy terms.

Retention

App usage events and installation records with no activity are scheduled for deletion after 180 days. Unsent analytics on your Mac are limited to seven days and 500 events; disabling analytics clears that queue immediately.

Your authentication account and spendable credit balance are retained while your account is active. On deletion, sign-in access and spendable credits are removed or disabled. Minimal payment, entitlement, fraud-prevention, and accounting records may be retained for applicable statutory periods and to reconcile refunds, disputes, or payments completed after deletion. Local reading data remains on your Mac until you remove it; iCloud and Google Drive copies remain under their respective accounts until removed there.

Account deletion and your rights

You can delete your account from the account menu. This does not delete reading data stored locally on your Mac and does not automatically request a payment refund.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a portable copy of personal data, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also complain to your competent data-protection authority. Contact olly@kodi-reader.app to exercise these rights.

Changes

We may update this policy when services or legal requirements change. The updated date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated through the app, website, or account email where appropriate.
