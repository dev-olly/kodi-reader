Kodi Reader

Privacy

Updated 26 September 2026.

Your reading stays local

Your library, annotations, and saved conversations stay on your Mac. You can read without an account. When you use Ask AI, your question, selected passages, relevant conversation history, and book context are sent through our server to OpenAI to generate your answer.

Sign-in

Supabase stores your account identifier and email address and manages authentication. Login codes are delivered through Purelymail. When enabled, Google or Apple handles its sign-in flow and shares the information needed to identify your account with Supabase; Apple may supply a private relay email. Session credentials are kept in macOS Keychain.

Account deletion

You can delete your account from the account menu. This removes sign-in access, while local books, notes, and conversations remain on your Mac.

We do not intentionally log login codes, access tokens, emails, questions, or book passages in server request logs. Authentication, AI, and email providers process the data needed to provide their services under their own policies.

Questions or deletion requests: olly@kodi-reader.app.
