# Kodi Reader 0.4.6

- Save one movable reading marker in each EPUB, PDF, or saved webpage. Click the beginning of a line, or press **M** and click the text where you stopped.
- In EPUBs, a faint flag appears at line starts on hover. The saved marker follows its text when typography changes.
- Press **Shift+M** or click **Return to marker** to jump back. Remove it from the marker toolbar menu; **Esc** cancels placement.
- Keep markers after reopening a document and, with sync enabled, across Macs. Reading progress and ordinary bookmarks stay independent, while links and text selection keep working normally.

The app and update feed are signed, and the download is notarized by Apple. Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later.
