# Kodi Reader 0.3.2

A smoother sign-in experience, a more spacious notes margin, and quieter updates.

- First launch opens a dedicated welcome page with Google, Apple, and email sign-in.
  Choose **Skip for now** to continue reading without an account.
- Ask AI requires sign-in. This release has no credit balances, payment prompts, or purchases.
- Google and Apple sign-in use a browser callback that safely restores the session.
- Account controls are available from the home screen and Ask AI.
- Search notes and open entry and chapter filters directly from the redesigned margin.
- Updates appear in the home and reader toolbars. Download when ready, then choose
  **Restart to update**; your reading progress is saved before restarting.

Your books, notes, drawings, and conversations remain on your Mac.
The app and update feed are signed, and the download is notarized by Apple.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later.
