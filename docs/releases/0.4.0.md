# Kodi Reader 0.4.0

This release prepares Ask AI for secure credit purchases while Kodi Reader itself
remains free.

- Ask AI now understands server-managed balances, credit packs, and one-credit
  answer charging.
- Checkout opens securely in the browser and returns to Kodi Reader after payment;
  the app then refreshes the balance automatically.
- Questions and selected passages remain available when sign-in or checkout is
  interrupted.
- Session refresh, sign-out, account switching, insufficient-credit responses, and
  service errors have clearer recovery behavior.
- Credit and purchase controls stay hidden until the production rollout is enabled.
  Reading, notes, drawings, and saved conversations continue to work as before.

The app and update feed are signed, and the download is notarized by Apple.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later.
