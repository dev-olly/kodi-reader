# Kodi Reader 0.4.3

- Rename EPUBs, PDFs, and saved webpages in your library: right-click a document and choose **Rename…**, or use **File → Rename Document…** while reading.
- Your chosen name stays after reopening and appears in the reading toolbar and Ask AI context. With sync enabled, it syncs as part of the book details.
- Renaming preserves your notes, drawings, bookmarks, conversations, and reading position. The original file keeps its name.

The app and update feed are signed, and the download is notarized by Apple. Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later.
