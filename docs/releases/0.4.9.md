# Kodi Reader 0.4.9

- Record audio notes alongside your highlights and text notes. Pause, resume,
  play back, rename, export, and manage recordings from the note editor.
- Listen to selected passages or the rest of the current English EPUB chapter
  with on-device read aloud, and transcribe audio notes locally. Optional voice
  files and speech-model setup happen when you use these features.
- Save research reminders tied to passages or pages, return to their notes,
  and keep reminders and audio attachments with your optional library sync.
- Search, sort, and filter your library more easily, including sorting by the
  date a document was added.
- Improve reader engagement analytics, meaningful note and drawing counts,
  Ask AI outcomes, and approximate connection-country reporting. A one-time
  notice and Settings → Privacy make usage sharing easy to turn off. Book text,
  note content, drawings, recordings, transcripts, and AI content are excluded
  from analytics.
- Keep note-sheet activity in engagement time, ignore idle time and canvas
  navigation, and avoid inflating feature counts with repeated autosaves.
- Keep deliberate page turns when closing or resizing the notes sidebar.
- Refine note editing, exports, syncing, and passage selection. The standalone
  web browser has been removed; existing saved web documents remain readable.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later. Includes iCloud and Google Drive
sync support. Update Kodi on every Mac before managing reminders and audio
attachments across devices. Local audio models require a separate download.
